Healthcare-Operational-Analytics-Dashboard

This project analyzes operational efficiency and patient flow for a healthcare facility using a dataset of 180 patient records from January through June 2026. The primary goal was to identify patterns in peak volume, departmental demand, and patient absenteeism to facilitate data-driven resource optimization and improved revenue cycle management.  

Technical Stack & Tools
Excel: Used for the initial data preparation, where raw records were cleaned, de-duplicated, and standardized across fields like insurance type and appointment classification.  

Pivot Tables & Statistical Charts: Leveraged to isolate temporal trends, peak arrival times, and departmental percentages.  

Power BI: Utilized to develop an interactive dashboard for ongoing monitoring, highlighting a 13.33% overall no-show rate.  

Key Findings
The analysis of the clinical dataset and associated visualizations in Depatment and Appointment Breakdown, NO Show Analysis, and Screenshot 2026-05-02 140928.png revealed several critical insights:  

Peak Demand: Patient arrivals consistently spike at 9:00 AM, 1:00 PM, and 5:00 PM.  

Absenteeism: Mondays experience the highest no-show rate at approximately 20%, with May identified as the highest no-show month overall.  

Departmental Volume: Cardiology is the highest-volume specialty with 33 recorded visits.  

Visit Duration: Endocrinology has the highest average visit duration at 37 minutes, compared to 32 minutes for Primary Care.  

Daily Volume: The practice maintains a steady average of 1.55 visits per day.  

Recommendations
Staffing: Align administrative and clinical staff with the peak 9:00 AM, 1:00 PM, and 5:00 PM windows.  

Outreach: Implement targeted SMS and phone reminders for Monday and Saturday appointments to mitigate high no-show rates.  

Planning: Review scheduling capacity in Cardiology and investigate documentation workflows in Endocrinology to improve visit efficiency.  

Monitoring: Establish quarterly reviews to identify and prepare for seasonal surges.
